Task 1 · 15 marks

Message Makeover

Scenario. You're the student lead of your university's International Students Club. You'll give a 5-minute talk to first-year international students on “Why time management decides your first semester abroad.” Your teammate's draft deck uses weak topic titles. Fix it.

A. Turn topic titles into message titles

6 marks

Use the data under each title. A message title states the takeaway in full, specifically and clearly enough to “get it” in 3 seconds.

Results
Club survey: 72% of 150 first-years missed at least one deadline in month one.
0 words
Attendance
Study-planner workshop attendance rose from 40 to 95 after moving it to evenings.
0 words
Budget
Club spent ₹18,000 of ₹25,000 this term; ₹7,000 is left for the spring fest.
0 words

B. Write an opening hook

4 marks

Your first 15–20 seconds. Grab attention, state the topic and show why it matters. Avoid “Good morning, today I will talk about…”.

0 words

C. Write a closing with a call to action

3 marks

End with impact: a memorable line and a specific next step. “Thank you” on its own scores zero.

0 words

D. Set your objective

2 marks

Complete the sentence from Step 1 of “Set Your Objectives”. Choose one verb, then make it specific.

“After my talk, the audience should ”

How Task 1 is marked

  • 3 message titles: specific finding + full statement6
  • Hook: question, statistic or “imagine” opener; no generic start; right length4
  • Closing: clear next step (action verb) and not just “thank you”3
  • Objective: one verb + a specific outcome2
Tip from the lesson: “Sales Data” is a topic. “Sales grew 20% after the campaign” is a message. Copying the lesson's examples scores zero.
Task 2 · 15 marks

Build a 5-Slide University Presentation

Build a short deck for a first-year seminar at your future university. Use any tool from the session (Gamma, Canva, Google Slides, Slidesgo, Pitch…), but you own the message. Review every AI-generated slide.

1. Pick your topic

2. Your deck must have

  • 5 to 7 slides, including title and closing
  • An opening hook: a question or a striking number
  • Message titles, not topic titles
  • No more than 5 short points per slide
  • At least 2 slides with a relevant image or chart
  • A closing takeaway with a call to action
  • Speaker notes on at least 3 slides
  • Readable colours: dark text on light, or the reverse

3. Upload your deck
